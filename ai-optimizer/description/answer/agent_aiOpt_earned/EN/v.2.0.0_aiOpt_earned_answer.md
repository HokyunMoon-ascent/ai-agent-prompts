## 3) Channel-by-Channel Activation Strategy

The strategy is to accumulate two axes of signal as primary on the Review Sites category — external review RTBs and the left/right pair scenario — while in the Community category the same RTBs should form as supporting signals via spontaneous user reviews and official-account Q&A, with recommended expression patterns differing per channel according to channel role (lived experience / colloquial language / third-party verification).

:::accordion{title="Channel Strategy Check"}
**➊ Review Sites — Primary RTB**

- **Target Media Candidates**: :k[rtings.com], :k[laptopmag.com], :k[다나와], :k[BestReviews], :k[Ergonomic Trends]
- **Required RTB**: :k[건초염이 없어졌다], :k[carpal tunnel pain 줄었다], :k[손목/팔 피로가 줄었다], :k[좌/우 각각 모델 있음], :k[Lift] left-hand / right-hand pair
- **Channel Role**: Lived experience (real-use experiential language — comparable data + cumulative user ratings)
- **Recommended Expression Patterns**: "After two months of use, my tenosynovitis pain visibly decreased" / "Placing :k[Lift] as a left/right pair at two seats keeps my grip from breaking when I switch hands" (attribute·context-bearing — standalone abstract evaluatives "best" / "good" forbidden)
- **Content Format**: Use only honest activation formats — providing third-party-verifiable data, recruiting experience-group reviewers, commissioning specialist-media reviews — so that the brand's lineup appears tied to pain-reduction RTBs and the left/right pair scenario on external review·comparison pages (guide with attribute·context-bearing sentences)
- **Certification·Transparency Requirements**: Experience-group / sponsored reviews must disclose sponsorship / comparison data must disclose its source / no paid ratings or self-authored reviews / brand identification must be stated for official replies
- **Separation Rationale**: Third-party-verified RTBs accumulated on external media cannot be absorbed into the brand's own pages, and the pattern of AI Responses citing external media as their primary RTB source is consistently observed in Responses 1, 2, and 3

**➋ Community — Supporting RTB**
